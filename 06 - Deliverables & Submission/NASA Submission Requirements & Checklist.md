## 📌 Submission Portal Fields (Due before Hackathon Clock Expires)
- [ ] **Project Title:** *SPHEREx Hunter: Interactive Time-Domain Sky Browser & Citizen Science Discovery Engine*
- [ ] **Tagline / Short Summary (under 100 words):**
  > An open-source, web-based time-domain astronomy tool that transforms NASA's SPHEREx 102-band near-infrared survey into an interactive blink comparator and difference-imaging engine. Enables researchers and citizen scientists to enter any sky coordinates, detect moving asteroids and ultra-cool brown dwarfs (like WISE 0855), analyze 102-channel infrared spectra, and search for Planet X.

## 🏆 Scoring Rubric Alignment
- **Impact:** Solves the real big-data problem of SPHEREx (>1 billion objects) by enabling citizen science.
- **Creativity:** Co-adds 6 detector arrays to maximize SNR, and provides on-the-fly 102-band molecular spectra.
- **Validity:** Mathematically accurate Astropy WCS alignment, difference imaging, and validated on all 5 NASA benchmark targets.
- **Relevance:** Directly fulfills every primary and secondary requirement of the SPHEREx challenge.
