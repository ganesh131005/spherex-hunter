# 🎬 Demo Video Script & Storyboard (90-Second Version)
> **Format:** Screen capture with clear, confident voiceover narration and subtle upbeat background music.

---

## ⏱️ Video Timeline & Script

### [00:00 - 00:15] The Hook & Problem
- **Visual:** Fast zoom into the celestial sphere with SPHEREx mission render.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Since May 2025, NASA's SPHEREx mission has been mapping the entire sky every six months in 102 bands of near-infrared light. With over one billion objects observed across four epochs, hidden asteroids, ultra-cool brown dwarfs, and even Planet X are waiting to be found. But no one person can sift through all that data alone."*

### [00:15 - 00:35] Introducing SPHEREx Hunter
- **Visual:** Open browser at `spherex-hunter.app`. Type coordinates into the search bar. Show smooth responsive UI.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Meet SPHEREx Hunter, an open-source, web-based time-domain astronomy tool created by Team RIP. Users simply enter sky coordinates or pick a benchmark target to instantly stream multi-epoch imagery aligned to sub-pixel accuracy."*

### [00:35 - 00:55] The Blink Comparator & Difference Imaging in Action
- **Visual:** Click "WISE 0855". Show the blink animation playing at 3 FPS. The cold brown dwarf shifts across the frame. Then click "Difference Mode"—a clear dipole pops up!
- **Narration:**
  > *"Our interactive blink comparator mimics the classic technique Clyde Tombaugh used to discover Pluto, utilizing our brain's motion-detection vision. With our difference imaging toggle, static background stars vanish, leaving only moving objects and variable stars clearly highlighted."*

### [00:55 - 01:15] 102-Band Spectral Analysis & Co-addition
- **Visual:** Click the target to open the 102-band spectral curve. Highlight the $H_2O$ and $CO_2$ absorption features.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Unlike traditional optical tools, SPHEREx Hunter leverages all 102 infrared channels. Users can co-add detector arrays to boost signal-to-noise by four times, or click any pixel to generate an on-the-fly spectrum, instantly revealing atmospheric water vapor and methane."*

### [01:15 - 01:30] Call to Action & Conclusion
- **Visual:** Show URL deep-link copy button, the Flag Candidate modal, and the Team RIP outro slide.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Every discovery is deep-linkable and ready for citizen science validation. Developed for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge by Team RIP in Coimbatore, India. Explore the infrared sky with SPHEREx Hunter today!"*
